EPMOS Founder Dashboard

A founder dashboard built on receipts, not claims.

Every row on an EPMOS Founder Dashboard is a receipt of work actually done: a post URL or ID, a store identifier, a filed document, or a verified check. If a number is not backed by a receipt, it shows as $0 / unknown, plainly. Nothing is invented: no sales, reach, or engagement figures without evidence behind them.

What a client receives

  • Your own dashboard, in your own folder. It shows only your receipts — never another client's data.
  • A live receipts feed. Work published, listings live, and filings made, each with its identifier or link.
  • Governance included. Permission-based access, capability separated from authority, and a sealed audit trail. Governance is part of the package, not an add-on tier.
  • Written permission first. Nothing is published or filed for you without your written approval.
