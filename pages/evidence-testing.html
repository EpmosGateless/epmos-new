EXPLANATION

Testing philosophy

Mature engineering does not pretend failures did not occur. A useful record preserves the question, result, failure, repair, exact retest, widened regression, and the limits of the conclusion.

Evidence categories

Validated results, known failures, repairs, regression tests, and independent reproduction remain distinguishable. A successful internal run is not silently converted into proof it did not earn.

Security boundary

Protected test inputs, exploit details, credentials, private prompts, controlled source, and architecture-revealing weaknesses remain outside the public portal.
