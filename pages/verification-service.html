EPMOS VERIFICATION SERVICE

Bring us your AI. We attack it before production does.

A standalone adversarial qualification service for AI agents, model-enabled workflows, and autonomous systems that need to prove how they behave before they receive consequential production authority.

MASTER · GOLDEN-DERIVED · ZERO-TOLERANCE QUALIFICATION
THE QUESTION WE TEST

Can it do the job?

Traditional testing often asks whether a system can complete a task. That matters, but it is not enough for autonomous systems.

Will it stay inside authority?

EPMOS Verification tests whether capability remains bounded by permission, identity, scope, lifecycle, evidence, provenance, and explicit control conditions.

What happens under pressure?

We deliberately exercise failure conditions, conflicting state, revoked or expired authority, malformed controls, scope substitution, lifecycle violations, and other adversarial conditions designed to expose governance escapes.

WHAT YOU GET
01

Adversarial qualification

A governed test run designed to expose where an AI system breaks boundaries, misuses authority, or fails unsafe.

02

Failure evidence

Failures are preserved as evidence rather than erased, so remediation can be tied to the exact condition that exposed the defect.

03

Retest discipline

Repairs are expected to survive the exact original failure and then wider regression pressure. Tests are not weakened to manufacture a pass.

04

Qualification record

Runs are designed around identified targets, case accounting, family coverage, custody, reproducibility, and explicit limits on what the evidence proves.

THE BASELINE

Adversarial governance families

The current MASTER service is derived from Golden and exercises the complete canonical Golden adversarial family set rather than a small demo checklist.

Zero material escape tolerance

A material governance escape is not averaged away. The qualification standard is zero tolerance for material governance escapes within the defined test contract.

High-volume qualification

The service is qualified through high-volume adversarial testing, including an independent clean-run reproduction, with zero failures in its qualified contract.

Qualified engine, separate target verdicts

The Verification Service itself is MASTER. That does not mean every system submitted to it is safe. Every outside target must be separately identified, adapted, tested, and evidenced. A target receives only the conclusion its own evidence earns.

INDUSTRY-SPECIFIC PRESSURE

One governance floor

The base EPMOS verification floor does not weaken by industry. Golden and GATELESS remain above the service in the EPMOS authority hierarchy.

Domain packs can get harder

Healthcare, finance, defense, robotics, cybersecurity, and other sectors can add specialized attack families, regulatory scenarios, tool boundaries, and consequence models without changing the constitutional governance floor.

No vanity testing

More cases are useful only when they add meaningful attack diversity, state interaction, failure coverage, or independent evidence. Volume alone is not treated as quality.
