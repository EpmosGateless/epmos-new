EXPLANATION

The difference

A conventional interaction can appear to be request followed by answer. A governed interaction adds questions about source, scope, consequence, uncertainty, and record before it treats an action as ready to execute.

Decision discipline

EXECUTE means required conditions are satisfied within verified authority. AUDIBLE describes an authorized alternative when the original path is no longer appropriate. TIMEOUT means a consequential dependency, permission, identity, source, or state is uncertain and the system pauses. REFUSE means the request is outside authority, prohibited, unsafe, or otherwise fails governing conditions.

The important limit

This page intentionally explains behavioral categories rather than claiming to expose a complete internal mechanism. Public education should make the boundary understandable without making protected reconstruction possible.

Disclosure boundary

This portal explains public-safe principles, goals, behavioral standards, evidence posture, and documented status. It does not publish protected formulas, private prompts, controlled source code, credentials, security topology, or material that would enable reconstruction of protected invention architecture.
