What happens next

The public intake creates a request package for the governed Factory intake stage. Canonical Factory IDs are assigned only after receipt and requester-authority verification. No public intake grants remediation, production mutation, credential change, release, or promotion authority.

WHAT TO SEND
01

YOUR SYSTEM

Name, version, model or agent type, and the workflow you want qualified.

02

INTENDED AUTHORITY

What the AI would be allowed to read, write, approve, send, deploy, modify, move, or control.

03

TOOLS & CONNECTIONS

APIs, databases, credentials, applications, infrastructure, or physical systems within scope. Do not submit credentials or secrets through public intake.

04

CONSEQUENCES

What can go wrong: financial, clinical, legal, security, operational, safety, privacy, or reputational impact.
