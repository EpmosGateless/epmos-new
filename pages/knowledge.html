GLOSSARY
EPMOS
A public-safe governance architecture and systems initiative focused on accountable intelligent capability.
GATELESS
An EPMOS-governed project / operating environment described according to its documented status.
Capability
The technical ability to perform an action.
Authority
Permission to perform an action under defined conditions.
Provenance
The lineage of a statement, record, source, or inference.
Evidence
What the available record actually establishes.
Uncertainty
A meaningful limit or unresolved condition that should remain visible.
Escalation
Moving a decision or action to an authorized human or stronger control.
Refusal
Declining a request because it is outside authority, prohibited, unsafe, or otherwise not governable.
Validation
Evidence that a defined claim or behavior has been tested against stated criteria.
Regression test
A test retained to check that a previously repaired failure does not return.
Bounded autonomy
Delegated action within explicit scope, conditions, and revocation paths.
FAQ
Is EPMOS an AI model?

No. EPMOS is presented as a governance architecture and systems initiative, not as a single model.

Is EPMOS a chatbot?

No. A conversational interface could be one surface, but it is not the definition of EPMOS.

Does EPMOS eliminate human control?

No. The public-safe position is that human governing authority remains defined, scoped, and revocable.

Can EPMOS make decisions autonomously?

The public record does not authorize a blanket claim of autonomous decision-making. Consequential authority requires explicit conditions and evidence.

Has EPMOS been independently validated?

The current public record does not claim independent validation unless a specific external record is published.

Is EPMOS commercially available?

The current public record does not establish broad commercial availability.

Why preserve failures?

Because a failure can become repair and regression evidence instead of disappearing from the record.

Disclosure boundary

This portal explains public-safe principles, goals, behavioral standards, evidence posture, and documented status. It does not publish protected formulas, private prompts, controlled source code, credentials, security topology, or material that would enable reconstruction of protected invention architecture.
