Love — First Material-Change Examination

This is a public-safe summary of the preserved first examination. It gives the proposition, test controls, expected outcomes, observed outputs, and verification identifiers while withholding protected implementation details.

Frozen proposition

Starting from legitimate standing, can the implementation distinguish between a material change that defeats the basis for proceeding and a material change that occurs while that basis remains intact?

Paired controls

Case A: material change defeats the basis for proceeding. Expected result: HOLD. Observed result: HOLD.

Case B: material change occurs while the basis for proceeding remains intact. Expected result: ADMISSIBLE. Observed result: ADMISSIBLE.

Falsifiers

Case A would fail if the system allowed the consequence after the basis was defeated. Case B would fail if the system imposed a hold even though the basis remained intact.

Observed result

False allowances: 0. False holds: 0. Overall: PASS.
