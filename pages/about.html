EXPLANATION

A public-safe description

EPMOS addresses a fundamental problem in advanced AI: capability alone is not authority. Powerful intelligent systems require evidence, boundaries, provenance, accountability, testing, recoverability, and human-governed authority.

The Founding Architect

The Founding Architect may be referenced as the responsible origin point for the initiative. This public site does not invent staff, offices, partnerships, certifications, customers, funding, affiliations, or endorsements.

Current maturity

The website and its public portal implementation are living and working. That status describes an active public implementation; it does not promote the institution or its protected systems to MASTER status.

Disclosure boundary

This portal explains public-safe principles, goals, behavioral standards, evidence posture, and documented status. It does not publish protected formulas, private prompts, controlled source code, credentials, security topology, or material that would enable reconstruction of protected invention architecture.
